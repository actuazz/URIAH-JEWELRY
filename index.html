Material & craft

Set under the microscope.

Our pavé is micro pavé: every small diamond is set by hand, at the bench, under a stereo microscope.

Visit the atelier

  1. Set under magnification

    Regular pavé is set by eye or with a loupe. Micro pavé is set under a stereo microscope, usually at 10 to 40 times, so the setter can work in fractions of a millimetre.

  2. Smaller stones, finer beads

    The tiny beads of metal that hold each diamond almost disappear, so the stones seem to float.

  3. Tighter tolerances

    Every stone must sit level, evenly spaced and secure. Under magnification a crooked stone is plain to see, and a loose one would eventually fall out.

  4. A specialist's work

    Micro setting takes years of practice. Fine jewellery houses in Europe, the US and Asia use it for their finest pieces; it is what the luxury market expects of serious craftsmanship.
